01 — Fleet & Vehicle Management · 2024 – 25

LA-ERP

A production fleet ERP for a logistics operator — trips, drivers, fuel, inspections, maintenance, and live GPS — replacing manual spreadsheets with one operational system.

01 The problem

A logistics operator running multi-vehicle operations on manual spreadsheets — no real-time visibility into where vehicles were, records scattered across files, and nothing reconciled. The kind of setup where a single missed update costs a day.

02 The approach

Map the operational workflow end-to-end — trips, drivers, fuel, inspections, maintenance — then build on proven infrastructure: Traccar for GPS, Appwrite for the backend, NocoDB as the data layer. Alerts go through the WhatsApp Business API the team already lives in, so adoption needs no new habits.

03 What I built

Trip management, driver records, fuel logging, vehicle inspections, and maintenance scheduling — with real-time GPS tracking via a Traccar integration, WhatsApp Business API alerts, and support for multi-vehicle operations, all in one system.

04 The outcome

Full operational visibility across the fleet, replacing manual spreadsheet tracking — a single source of truth the operations team runs on every day.

Stack Traccar Appwrite NocoDB WhatsApp Business API GPS

02 — Field Worker Safety & Dispatch · 2024

TKGM

A command-centre dashboard for field-worker safety and dispatch — real-time location awareness and coordination in one console, built as a white-label product for operations teams.

01 The problem

Field teams working in the open with no real-time picture of where people are or how to coordinate a response — a safety and dispatch gap where minutes matter and there was no shared view to act on.

02 The approach

A command-centre dashboard built as a white-label product, with a document-style interface and ink-line cartography tuned for operations teams rather than consumers — clear, calm, and readable under pressure.

03 What I built

Real-time location awareness, dispatch coordination, and survey/incident reporting — brought together in one operations console so a dispatcher can see, decide, and respond from a single screen.

04 The outcome

Real-time situational awareness for field teams, ready for enterprise white-label deployment — a console an operations team can put their own name on.

03 — EdTech & Cloud Platform · 2022 – now

TechGetAfrica

A technical-education platform built end-to-end — a Moodle LMS with a booking system, deployed on Azure and self-hosted on a homelab — now in partnership with Strathmore University.

01 The problem

A gap in accessible, practical technical education across East Africa — and no platform to deliver it at scale. Demand was real; the infrastructure to meet it was not.

02 The approach

Found and build a technical-education platform end-to-end — courses in web development, cloud, Cisco/Google certifications and AI — on a Moodle LMS with a booking system, deployed on Azure and self-hosted on a homelab server for cost and control.

03 What I built

The Moodle-based LMS, a booking system, and the full infrastructure stack — Azure plus a self-hosted homelab, with Docker, Traefik, and Cloudflare holding the deployment together.

04 The outcome

Active learners across Kenya, and a partnership with Strathmore University — a platform that runs in production and keeps growing.

Stack Moodle Azure Docker Traefik Cloudflare
