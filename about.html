03 — About

Six years building software that operations teams depend on every day.

I've spent six years building software that operations teams depend on every day — fleet dispatch systems, real-time GPS tracking platforms, field-worker safety dashboards, and enterprise ERPs.

I work end-to-end: from understanding your operational problem through to architecture, build, and handoff. I'm comfortable talking to procurement, engineering teams, and end users in the same week.

Based in Nairobi, I've shipped production systems for clients across East Africa and work remotely with international organisations that need reliable software built without unnecessary overhead.

How I work

Senior hands,
no overhead.

The way I work is the product as much as the software — predictable, owned end-to-end, and built to keep running after I hand it over.

01

End-to-end ownership

One person accountable from the operational problem through architecture, build, and handoff — so nothing falls between a designer, a contractor, and a vendor. You get a working system, not a pile of tickets.

02

Fluent across the room

Comfortable talking to procurement, engineering teams, and the end users who live in the system every day — often in the same week. Decisions get made faster when one person speaks all three languages.

03

Reliability-first

I build for the teams whose software can't go down — dispatch, tracking, safety. That means proven infrastructure, sensible defaults, and systems that behave the same on a bad day as a good one.

04

Built without unnecessary overhead

No bloated teams, no rebuilds for their own sake. The right tools for your operation, a clear scope, and a system your team can afford to run and actually owns at the end.
