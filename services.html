How I can help
your operation

From a single operational problem to a full system — designed, built, and handed over. Engagements that fit how your team actually works.

01

System design & build

End-to-end delivery: understanding the operational problem first, then architecture, build, and handoff. The point isn't a clever codebase — it's a system your team owns and runs without me in the loop.

What that can look like
  • Mapping the real workflow before any code
  • A clear architecture you and your team can stand behind
  • A built, working system handed over with documentation
02

Fleet & GPS platforms

Real-time tracking, dispatch, and fleet management built on proven infrastructure such as Traccar — the kind of system an operation depends on every working day, not a demo that falls over in the field.

What that can look like
  • Live vehicle and asset tracking on proven infrastructure
  • Dispatch and trip coordination for an operations team
  • Fleet records — drivers, fuel, inspections, maintenance
03

Real-time dashboards & integrations

Operations dashboards, WhatsApp and API integrations, and data tooling that turn scattered information into decisions your team can act on — so the people running the operation can see what's happening in one place.

What that can look like
  • A single operations dashboard pulling from your systems
  • WhatsApp and API integrations into existing tools
  • Data tooling that turns raw activity into decisions
04

Technical advisory

Architecture review, system audits, and consulting retainers — a senior pair of hands to de-risk the decisions before they get expensive. Often the cheapest hour you'll spend on a build is the one that stops the wrong one.

What that can look like
  • An architecture review before you commit to a direction
  • A system audit of what you already have running
  • An ongoing advisory retainer for senior input on call
